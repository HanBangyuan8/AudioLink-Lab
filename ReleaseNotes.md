Major release.

- Added a native macOS SwiftUI workflow for WAV latency analysis with deterministic signals, explicit preprocessing, and typed audio units.
- Added direct/FFT correlation, quality diagnostics, waveform and correlation visualization, SQLite history, repeated measurements, calibration, and clock drift analysis.
- Added same-Mac realtime loopback, Core Audio capability models, Bonjour/LAN protocol foundations, a native iOS companion, and distributed measurement foundations.
- Added versioned JSON/CSV/HTML/PDF/PNG reports, checksummed bundles, the `audiolink` CLI, localhost automation, adaptive planning, spatial IR, device benchmark, plugin, and signal-path foundations.
- Switched the project license to Mozilla Public License 2.0 and documented hardware, hostile-LAN, third-party Audio Unit, signing, notarization, and validation limitations.
